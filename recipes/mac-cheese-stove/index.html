Stovetop Mac & Cheese

Creamy homemade mac without the boxed mix — still budget-friendly.

4 servings25 minmediumcomfortkid-friendlyvegetarianunder-$9

Ingredients

  • Elbow macaroni16 oz
  • Cheddar cheese8 oz
  • Milk1 cup
  • Butter2 tbsp
  • Flour2 tbsp
  • Salt0.5 tsp

Steps

  1. Cook macaroni; drain.
  2. Melt butter, whisk in flour 1 minute, then slowly add milk.
  3. Stir in cheese until melted; fold in pasta.
  4. Season and serve immediately.
