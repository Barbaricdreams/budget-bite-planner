Chicken Noodle Dump Soup

Dump broth, noodles, canned chicken, and frozen veg into one pot.

2 servings20 mineasysoupone-potproteinunder-$6

Ingredients

  • Chicken broth4 cup
  • Egg noodles or broken spaghetti4 oz
  • Canned chicken10 oz
  • Frozen mixed vegetables1 cup
  • Salt0.5 tsp

Steps

  1. Bring broth to a boil; add noodles and frozen veg.
  2. Simmer until noodles are tender.
  3. Stir in drained canned chicken; heat through and season.
